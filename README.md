### 🔬 Academic & Research Focus

I am deeply interested in the intersection of **applied machine learning, systems engineering, and open research**. My goal is to build practical applications, explore complex systems, and bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and real-world engineering.

- 🌍 **Climate Resilience:** Exploring spatiotemporal deep learning to extend storm surge prediction into a real-time early warning system.
- 🗣️ **Low-Resource NLP:** Developing lightweight, on-device NLP models for code-switched Filipino languages (Taglish, Bislish).
- 🏥 **Medical AI:** Collaborating with clinicians to validate and deploy AI-assisted morphology analysis in low-cost settings.
- 🎓 **Education Tech:** Creating adaptive, open-source educational tools (like Edugate).
